## 1. Licensure & Associate Clinical Services
- **Price:** from **$200**
- **Target audience:** Pre-licensed Associate Clinicians (AMFT, ASW, APCC) and early-career mental-health professionals.
- **Included:** State board licensure roadmapping, hours-tracking strategy, board exam prep, supervision navigation, post-licensure career design.
- **Why it exists:** Bureaucratic friction during licensure causes early-career drop-off. Structured pathways to launch a clinical career.

## 2. Executive Leadership Program
- **Price:** from **$250**
- **Target audience:** Mid-level to C-suite executives, healthcare directors, transitioning clinicians.
- **Included:** Strategic career mapping, executive branding, 1:1 leadership coaching, interview/negotiation strategy, "The Unmasked Method" frameworks.
- **Why it exists:** Traditional career advice ignores the nuances of clinical expertise + authentic leadership. Bridges technical mastery with executive presence.

## 3. PsychoEd Workshops & Resources
- **Price:** Digital toolkits **$29–$99** · Live workshop registration **$150/attendee**
- **Target audience:** Enterprise teams, academic programs, self-directed learners.
- **Included:** Digital toolkits, asynchronous learning modules, live professional-development workshops, continuing-education content.
- **Why it exists:** Democratize high-impact clinical and career frameworks at scale.

## 4. Neurodivergent & Skill-Affirmative Coaching
- **Price:** **$950** (4-session package)
- **Target audience:** ADHD professionals, autistic leaders, neurodivergent executives.
- **Included:** Executive-functioning toolkits, workplace-accommodations consulting, unmasking strategies, strength-aligned workflow design.
- **Why it exists:** Standard corporate environments penalize non-linear thinkers. Build sustainable systems that work *with* cognitive strengths, not against them.

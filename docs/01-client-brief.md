**Tyler Suran, MA, APCC (he/him)** — California-registered **Associate Professional Clinical Counselor**, **Certified Drug & Alcohol Counselor**, PhD candidate in **Counselor Education & Supervision at The Chicago School**.

**Credentials (real, verified from his own sites):**

## The brand — Unmasked Career Lab

- **Tagline / core idea:** *"Drop the Mask."*
- **One-line positioning:** career advancement for people the mental-health system and corporate world were never built for — clinicians and neurodivergent leaders — without sacrificing who they are.
- **The problem it names:** the **"hidden curriculum"** — the unwritten rules, unspoken power dynamics, and systemic friction of clinical/executive career advancement that grad school never teaches.

## Who it serves (three audiences — keep a clear hierarchy)

1. **Pre-licensed associate clinicians** (AMFT, ASW, APCC) — licensure roadmapping, hours tracking, board exam prep, supervision navigation.
2. **Mid-career / burned-out clinicians** — pivot, master the business side of mental health, step into leadership.
3. **Neurodivergent & ADHD professionals/leaders** — executive-function coaching, unmasking, strength-aligned workflow design.

## Six core values (anchor every page's messaging)

1. Authenticity over masking — true authority comes from leading as your full self, not shrinking to fit.
2. Read the power dynamics — you can't navigate a game if you don't know the implicit rules.
3. Burnout is a systemic failure, not a badge of honor.
4. Your clinical background is your greatest strength — translate mastery into the language of business.
5. Human-centered leadership — high standards AND deep emotional safety at the same time.
6. No empty clichés, no toxic positivity — real-world results aligned with your ethics.

## Tone of voice (lock this — it's the differentiator)

- **Radical but warm.** Direct, no fluff. "We got you" energy, not "we'll fix you."
- **Anti-toxic-positivity.** Never "transform in 30 days." Never "just use a planner." Name the system, don't blame the person.
- **Speaks to the exhausted and fed-up.** Professionals who've been failed by rigid systems, masking, and neurotypical advice.
- **Slightly bitter edge.** It can be pointed about the industry — that's the brand's authenticity, not a flaw.
- **Academic weight, plain language.** Tyler rejects "performative jargon." Say hard things clearly.

## What must NOT happen

- ❌ Too much "about Tyler" up top — the brand must stand alone.
- ❌ Generic Squarespace-template filler (the current site literally has "fine-tune nutrition" on a career-coaching page).
- ❌ Aggressive "book now and be transformed" sales framing.
- ❌ Losing the legal disclaimer (consulting ≠ therapy).
